Privacy Policy

How Bunifu Digital Agency handles your personal information.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Bunifu Digital Agency ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Who we are

Bunifu Digital Agency is a digital marketing and creative agency based in Kenya. You can reach us at bunifudigitalagency@gmail.com or on 0722 822 256.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you contact us, ask for a free audit or hire us, you may share your name, phone number, email address, business details and your message.
  • The free audit form. This website has no database. The form only prepares a message that opens in WhatsApp, and you decide whether to send it. We receive the information only if you send it.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, so the website can be delivered and kept secure.
  • Fonts. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when a page loads.

This website does not currently use advertising cookies or analytics tools. If we add them, we will update this policy.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and prepare quotes
  • To deliver the services you ask for and manage our work with you
  • To keep our website secure and working properly
  • To meet legal and tax obligations

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers we need to run our business, such as WhatsApp (Meta), Google (Gmail and Google Fonts) and Vercel (hosting), or when the law requires it. These providers have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages and project information for as long as needed to deal with your request, deliver our services and meet legal obligations. After that we delete it or remove anything that identifies you.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent. To make a request, email bunifudigitalagency@gmail.com and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

Links to other sites

This website links to other services such as WhatsApp. We are not responsible for how those sites handle personal data.

Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email bunifudigitalagency@gmail.com or call 0722 822 256.
