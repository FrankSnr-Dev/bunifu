Five social media mistakes small businesses make

23 October 2026 · 4 min read

← All articles

Almost every small business has a social media page. Far fewer have a page that brings in enquiries. When a page is quiet, the cause is usually one of these five mistakes.

1. Posting without a goal

Every post should have one job: help people discover you, help them trust you, or help them buy. A page that only shares good morning graphics collects followers who never become customers. Before you post, ask what you want the viewer to do next.

2. Leaving comments and messages unanswered

Customers often ask for the price in the comments or send a quick direct message. If nobody replies for two days, they buy from someone else. Check your messages at least twice a day, answer in the comments, and move serious buyers to WhatsApp to close the sale.

3. Using a different look every time

Different fonts, colours and logo positions make a business look unreliable. Pick two brand colours, two fonts and a simple post template. When people see the same look repeatedly, they start to recognise you before they read your name.

4. Posting in bursts

Ten posts in one day followed by three silent weeks does not build trust. Steady posting shows customers that you are active and open for business. Three or four good posts a week is easier to keep up and works better than a rush of content.

5. Never checking what worked

Likes feel nice but they do not pay rent. Once a month, look at which posts led to messages, saves, shares or sales, and make more of those. Drop the formats that get no response.

Start small. Pick one of the five and fix it this week. A clear goal for each post is a good place to begin.
